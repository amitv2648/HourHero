HourHero

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

HourHero helps students log volunteer hours and connect with organizations. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your name and email address when you sign up.
  • Volunteer activity including logged hours, submissions, and joined opportunities.
  • Organization details and opportunities posted by organization accounts.

How we use your information

  • To provide the HourHero service, including verifying volunteer hours.
  • To display leaderboards, badges, and organization dashboards.
  • To send account-related emails such as password resets when you request them.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your choices

You can update your display name and email in Settings. You may delete your account at any time from the account menu; this permanently removes your profile and associated data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us through the link on the HourHero landing page.
